## 👋 Welcome

**TimeLock** is a new mobile app launching in **Summer 2025**, designed to help you save personal messages, media, and milestones for future you (or loved ones). Think of it like a private time machine for your digital legacy.

We believe that memories matter. And in a fast-paced, content-saturated world — we want to slow things down and preserve what truly counts.

🔗 [Visit timelockapp.com →](https://timelockapp.com)

---

## 📲 Key Features
- Store private memories, letters, photos, and videos
- Lock content until a future date of your choice
- Share capsules with friends or keep them just for you
- Built with intention, privacy, and emotion in mind
